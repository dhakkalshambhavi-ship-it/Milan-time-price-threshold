# Why 200 Not 2000? - Milan Time Price Threshold

Twice a year, I live on Milan time. Watching Bvlgari and Cartier FW24, I paused at 14:32 - not for the look, but for the decision: How do they decide 200 pieces not 2000?

This repo tracks 8 luxury drops across Milan Fashion Week and models when desire breaks.

**Data:** Manual tracking of Bvlgari, Cartier, Chanel launches (Vogue Runway + Instagram)
**Finding:** Resale premium holds 35-55% at 85-200 pieces, crashes to 2-5% after 500 pieces. Optimal is 180-220.
**Stack:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib
